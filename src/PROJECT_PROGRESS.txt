STORAGE AND BEHAVIOR NOTES
-------------------------
- SD archive folders: /daily/YYYY-MM-DD/ and /monthly/YYYY-MM/.
- Daily and monthly CSV records are mirrored onto the same GPIO14 SD card.
- Legacy root CSV files are migrated during startup; the source is retained
  when migration fails.
- Slot taken/available state, user accounts, registered keys, active slot
  settings, and admin PIN are stored in ESP32 Preferences.
- Fingerprint templates remain in the fingerprint sensor.
- Current-log cards are reconstructed from today's SD transaction archive;
  transaction history/date folders are also restored from SD at startup.
- resetEverythingForActualProject() clears project data and must remain
  disabled in normal setup.
